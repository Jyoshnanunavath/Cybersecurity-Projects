Subdomain Enumeration

Runs entirely in your browser. Candidate names are checked against a public DNS-over-HTTPS resolver — no server, no logging on our end, nothing installed.

Authorized use only. Only run this against a domain you own, or one you have explicit written permission to assess. Enumerating subdomains of a domain you don't control, without permission, can violate computer-misuse law even though every technique here is passive and public.
